GET TO KNOW ME

More Than Just Code.

I'm a creative person who enjoys exploring the connection between technology, design, creativity, and the things that inspire me.

My name is Alexander Underhill, but I go by Zander. I'm from Vermont, and I'm passionate about technology, web design, graphic design, gaming, and digital media.

I graduated from Mount Mansfield Union High School in 2025. During high school, I studied Computer Animation & Web Design at the Center for Technology, Essex.

I'm currently an online student at Champlain College, pursuing a degree in Web Design. I enjoy combining creativity and technology to bring original ideas to life.
